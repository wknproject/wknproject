About this lab

By day, I think about customer journeys at the scale of millions. Here, it's the same instinct, just applied at the scale of one. Just an idea, and enough of a weekend to see it through.

Just the craft itself: notice something ordinary, imagine it a little better, build it, find out if it holds up.

Nothing here is polished for its own sake. Each experiment stays as close to the real, working version as I can show it, status included, results included, whether they worked or not.

Got something to experiment on together?

This lab is also where collaborative projects will get their own entries once they're underway. If you're thinking about building something and want a partner, reach out.
